## Title: Monday Coffee Market Analysis Capstone
## Problem statement: 
Monday Coffee, an online coffee brand operating across multiple Indian cities since January 2023, plans to expand into physical brick-and-mortar locations. 
However, opening physical stores involves high capital expenditure and market risk. 
The problem is that the company lacks a data-driven framework to identify which specific cities, neighborhoods, and customer demographics offer the highest potential for physical retail success. 
This project analyzes past online sales data, customer density, and regional purchasing power to pinpoint the optimal locations for expansion.
## Data description: 
The data comprises of four tables that is city, customers,products and sales which has a relationship between them.

## Key insights and recommendations
Cities with a low avg_rent_per_customer paired with a high avg_sale_per_customer represent high-margin markets. 
These areas should be prioritized over mega-cities where high rent completely drains customer lifetime value.

## Limitations and future work
The current setup utilizes descriptive analytics limiting predictive analysis.
